Weekly adjustments

Weekly changes are from DOE and oil company announcements (Oct 20, Oct 27, Nov 3, 2026). Unleaded (RON 91, ₱86.80) and Premium (RON 95, ₱87.70) start from DOE's Metro Manila common prices plus the Nov 3 hike. Premium is assumed to move the same as unleaded until you enter a different change. Diesel's starting price is an estimate, so enter your own station's price. The trend forecast is a simple estimate, not a guarantee. New weekly adjustments are fetched automatically, with GasWatch PH first and the DOE Oil Monitor when it's published (DOE's numbers take priority). Your own prices and settings stay on this device.
